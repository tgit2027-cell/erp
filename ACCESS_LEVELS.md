# Access levels (Master Admin down to Sales)

Sam asked for preset access levels plus custom users that only a Master Admin can create.
This page lists what each level gets. Everything marked **provisional** is a suggested default
used until Sam confirms or changes it.

## Who can manage people

| Who | Can do on Users and permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Master Admin | Everything: any level, custom users (permissions one by one), copying a colleague's access, teams, reporting and chat roles, other admins. |
| General Admin | Add people, put them on **Head, Manager or Sales**, switch accounts on or off, set temporary passwords, but only for people who are not admins or custom users. Sees everyone's permissions read-only. **Provisional.** |
| Everyone else | No access to the screen. |

Safety rules: you cannot take away your own Master Admin level, and there must always be at least one
other active Master Admin before one is changed. Every existing system administrator is shown as a Master Admin.

## What each level gets

Admin levels apply in **every business unit**. Head, Manager and Sales apply in the **business units chosen** for the person.

| Level | Gets (all provisional except Master Admin) |
| --- | --- |
| Master Admin | Every permission in every unit, both supplier permissions, every approval role (salesperson up to CEO), both chat review roles, and system administrator. |
| General Admin | Sales and stock, all warehouse work except approving write-offs, editing items (not item costs), staff records (not salaries), opening new suppliers. No finance, no payroll. |
| Finance Admin | All finance (payments, bank and cash, ledger, month close, bank reconciliation, expenses, budgets, fixed assets, journals, credit/debit notes, refunds), item costs, approving write-offs, seeing and approving payroll, approving new suppliers, reading stock. |
| HR Admin | Staff records, salaries and preparing payroll, posting on the HR Portal, and asking to review a chat. (Finance approves payroll, so no one person both prepares and approves it.) |
| Head | In chosen units: sales, stock, transfers (request and approve), approving stock counts, low-stock levels, item costs, the ledger (view), entering and approving expenses, preparing budgets, approving refunds, staff records (view). Approval role: business-unit GM. |
| Manager | In chosen units: sales, stock, transfers (request and approve), counting stock, low-stock levels, entering expenses. Approval role: manager. |
| Sales | In chosen units: see stock, make quotations and orders, ask for transfers. Approval role: salesperson. |
| Custom | Whatever a Master Admin switches on one by one. Changing a preset person one by one turns them into Custom and remembers the level they started from. |

## How it behaves

- Choosing a level **replaces** the permissions given to the person directly. Permissions they get from a team stay.
- Reporting roles and warehouse capacity access are not part of any level; they are still set separately.
- The Commercial GM and CEO approval roles are only given by Master Admin (or as part of Master Admin).
- A business unit added later is not added to existing admins automatically; re-apply their level to include it. **Provisional.**
