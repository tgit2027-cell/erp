---
name: Function-page navigation requirement
description: The owner's workspace organization means focused function views, not scroll-only sidebar anchors.
---

All implemented sidebar child destinations, including legacy Purchasing and
Reports management shortcuts, must open focused function views, not just scroll
within long shared pages. Favorites must support individual implemented functions
while preserving existing section pins. Do not claim that expandable grouping
alone completes this requirement.

**Why:** The owner clarified twice that child links opening one shared long page
did not satisfy the information architecture, and subsequently authorized
all-sections corrections rather than retaining legacy aggregate exceptions.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing authorized controls; keep browser history,
direct function links, BU context and section-level Favorites coherent.
Preserve mounted form drafts, pending outcomes and retry identities within a
workspace while hiding inactive functions from visual and keyboard access.
Use canonical management destinations and contextual links for legitimately
shared journeys. Do not invent functioning pages for incomplete sections.
Navigation completion is not production/business-policy acceptance.

Cross-section navigation must not silently discard local transaction drafts or
detach a pending submission from its outcome/retry identity. Same-workspace
function navigation should continue to preserve mounted state.

**Why:** The owner explicitly extended acceptance to leaving a business section
while editing or submitting, not just moving between its children.

**How to apply:** Require an explicit draft-discard decision, keep pending
submissions running, and prevent section changes until their outcomes settle.
Cover sidebar, Favorites and browser-history/direct-fragment navigation; never
add automatic transaction retries as a navigation recovery mechanism.
