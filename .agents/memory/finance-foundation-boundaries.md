---
name: Finance foundation boundaries
description: Owner-approved isolation and company boundaries for continuing Finance development before operational policy/access is established.
---

Finance planning uses one legal entity with business-unit dimensions, not a
presumed multi-company operation. Preserve all supplied Finance coverage and
beneficial additions rather than treating synthetic journals or reports as the
complete Finance section.

When no existing capability genuinely covers Finance, the owner permits an
isolated synthetic prototype and tests, not reuse of staff, superuser,
purchasing, reporting or warehouse authority to expose Finance to shared users.
Missing policy/access decisions block their dependent activation only; they
must not halt independent draft workflow development.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested continued Finance development while
forbidding inferred accounting policy or access assignments, and specified one
legal entity with BU dimensions.

**How to apply:** Keep unapproved posting/tax/FX/valuation/approval behavior
inactive, name each actual access dependency, and separate isolated prototype
evidence from shared-user authorization, team acceptance and production readiness.

Explicit synthetic lab-session access is permitted only in an independently
verified disposable environment, not as a bypass in the normal ERP URL
configuration. A real Django test session is not proof of ordinary Finance
password login, approved Finance capabilities or shared-user authority.

**Why:** The owner explicitly authorized UI-to-backend draft development using
synthetic test access while forbidding inferred production permissions.

**How to apply:** Keep normal app routes/access unchanged and identify the
actual authentication boundary in every acceptance claim. Shared exposure
still requires approved Finance scope and deliberate assignment.

HTTP/test-access isolation is not schema isolation: models in a shared installed
app participate in its normal migration-readiness requirements even when their
routes are disposable-only. Do not bypass that guard or infer shared schema
approval from authorization to build an isolated prototype.

**Why:** Isolated Finance development introduced pending migrations into the
shared app's migration graph and prevented normal preview startup.

**How to apply:** Assess migration coupling explicitly before extending isolated
schema. Keep shared upgrades separately reviewed, backup/restore-rehearsed and
authorized; continue independent disposable development without faking migration
history or silently weakening startup readiness.

For new disposable-only Finance schema, prefer a separate app installed only by
the guarded lab settings, rather than adding models/migrations to the shared
foundation app. Verify the normal migration graph excludes that app.

**Why:** Earlier draft additions in the shared app made isolated prototype schema
mandatory for preview startup; isolated URLs alone did not prevent that coupling.

Dates in non-posting proposals are explicit user inputs, not effective posting
dates, an approved fiscal calendar or a recurrence/recognition convention.

**Why:** The owner authorized explicit date/amount proposals while explicitly
withholding automatic recurrence, fiscal calendars/distribution, month-end
adjustment, FX and recognition rules.

**How to apply:** Keep proposal previews and user-entered scenario date envelopes
separate from fiscal periods, scheduling and operative journals. Obtain dependent
rules before activating generation or posting; missing rules do not block
isolated explicit drafts.

User-supplied location/reference text in Finance asset proposals does not confer
warehouse access or assign physical custody. Keep it distinct from authorized
physical-location records.

**Why:** The owner authorized isolated asset record drafts with optional
user-supplied location details while explicitly withholding custody authority
and shared Finance access decisions.

**How to apply:** Any later warehouse lookup/link must independently satisfy
existing warehouse/location authorization; lab access or a typed location name
is not an assignment, access grant or approved custody policy.

Forecast display sequencing and negative proposed balances must not be
interpreted as payment priority, execution timing, overdraft approval or actual
cash availability.

**Why:** The owner authorized explicit manual cash scenarios with same-date
ordering limited to presentation, while withholding inferred timing, bank
integration, approvals and posting.

**How to apply:** Keep later operational cash/AP/AR integration separately
authorized; a forecast proposal cannot establish realization or credit policy.

Manual Finance supplier/customer/document references do not authorize
counterparty master creation or procurement/customer-directory access.
Internal invoice proposals do not confer customer-document issuance or output
rights. Possible duplicate-reference warnings
are review aids, not an owner-approved uniqueness, merge or rejection policy.

**Why:** The owner explicitly separated isolated invoice proposals from supplier/
customer master authority, issued client documents and operational AP/AR, and requested duplicate flags without
silently merging records or adopting a company uniqueness rule.

**How to apply:** Keep future counterparty/AP/AR activation and duplicate enforcement
separately authorized; draft reference entry cannot grant supplier/bank access
or establish payment terms, invoice acceptance or settlement authority.

Supplier credit/debit and customer-credit proposal classification must not silently establish an
accounting sign convention, account mapping or payable/receivable effect. Keep explicit
proposal magnitudes separate from any future operational adjustment.

**Why:** The owner authorized isolated review drafts while explicitly withholding
sign inference, refunds/settlement, account mappings and operative balance changes.

**How to apply:** Never convert a credit label into a negative balance or infer
journal lines. Require approved dependent conventions before any operational
AP/AR integration; keep saved source-reference evidence distinct from a mutable
live invoice relationship.

Finance draft development should follow a finite requirements backlog and
consolidate existing usable workflows, rather than automatically turning every
unfinished operational requirement into another disconnected worksheet.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a finite consolidation backlog while
retaining full operational Finance scope, then prohibited inventing further
worksheets outside the approved requirements after its bounded candidates.

**How to apply:** Separate independent draft/correctness work from activation
dependencies, reconcile real reviewer needs before extending the list, and
never treat completed drafts as complete operational Finance.

Recovery claims must be bounded to the retained request identity and database,
not described as universal exactly-once processing. A pre-commit failure only
proves rollback; uncertainty acceptance needs evidence that commit preceded
lost acknowledgement and that authorized recovery/replay preserves the result.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required durable uncertain-create recovery while
forbidding claims beyond tested scope; the disposable lab intentionally loses
its database on exit.

**How to apply:** Distinguish original create acknowledgement from current
edited revision, identity/status metadata from a persisted form payload, and
isolated synthetic recovery from shared/production or disaster-recovery proof.

Shared Finance may describe isolated implementation status and link to existing
operational sections, but must not launch/link lab routes or imply that schema
readiness, staff, panel-manager or superuser status grants Finance authority.

**Why:** The owner authorized an integration-readiness/status page while expressly
keeping the lab isolated and forbidding new grants or shared activation.

**How to apply:** Keep operator-only lab review instructions separate from the
shared UI; label loopback access limitations and distinguish prototype evidence,
team acceptance and operational accounting.
