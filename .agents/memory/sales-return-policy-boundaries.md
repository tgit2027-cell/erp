Sales-return intake is non-posting. Capture the originating sale/items, inspection,
receipt versus large-item warehouse collection and proposed cash/card refund
preference, usually matching the recorded original method. Finance approval/review
must stay pending; do not infer authority or execute refunds, credits, stock
put-back or accounting/valuation reversals.

**Why:** The owner explicitly authorized intake before the outstanding Finance
sales-return decisions, not a financial return-processing policy.

**How to apply:** Future work must not turn inspection or a Purchasing role into
Finance approval. A not-good inspection has no approved disposition, exception or
eligibility exclusion.

The owner confirmed on 2026-10-19: **"Confirmed; count from delivery."** Enforce the
configurable default of 14 calendar days from the actual delivery date as a
company rule. This is not a verified Qatar-law statement; legal validation remains
separate. Do not use invoice or payment dates.

**Why:** The earlier unconfigured basis was intentional while approval was missing,
but the owner has now explicitly authorized delivery-based date-window validation.

**How to apply:** Date-window eligibility is not good-condition approval, Finance
approval or authorization for refunds/stock posting. Preserve those pending
decisions. Never overwrite historical audit or idempotency outcomes when
reassessing records that predate this confirmation.
