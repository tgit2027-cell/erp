# Later increments — scope reference only

1. Private executable organization, data and permission foundation: current work.
2. Financial/stock transaction services: immutable posted journals, decimal calculations, atomic stock updates and reservations, idempotency, rollback and real PostgreSQL concurrency tests. No broad UI yet.
3. Purchasing-to-delivery workflow: receipts, purchase-value landed allocation, pricing authority, quotes, discount approvals, holds, simulated deposits/payments, dispatch and reconciliation.
4. Restore/portability/load proof: restore documents and database into a clean environment, run outside Replit, benchmark agreed workload and assess production hosting.
5. Subsequent core workstreams: deeper Finance, HR/payroll, governance, delivery/after-sales, existing factory/project continuity and migration preparation. These are mandatory Phase 1 work, not fulfilled by the first demo.

Approved retail policy inputs for later work: discount limits salesperson 10%, manager 20%, commercial GM 30%, CEO exceptional 50%; temporary holds max three working days with Friday closed; confirmed stock reservation minimum 50% deposit; stock delivery normally paid in full; authorized exceptions recorded. Special orders have transaction-specific payment terms. Free storage one month from readiness notification then monthly charge based on total quote; apply exact policy from the foundation register before implementation. No new automatic discount bands, price multipliers or payroll deductions may be invented.

Before financial posting design, obtain Finance-accepted valuation/recognition/currency policy; synthetic tests may use explicit test-only assumptions. Before statutory payroll work, validate applicable rules and approved internal policies. AI monitoring comes after deterministic monitoring and restricted access exist.
